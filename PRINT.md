# Future Forge — print sheet

Everything below is ready for a printer. Copy is from the specification, verbatim. No official Clarkson marks are included: the wordmark line is a text placeholder pending brand approval — replace it with the approved lockup before printing.

The physical card decks are not part of this sheet. They are finished: print files, SVG masters and 450 dpi rasters live in the `future-forge-cards` output folder, and `claude/card-decks.md` in the project describes them (two nine-card decks, 4-inch square, black on white, numbers on the backs only). Do not redesign them here.

---

## 1. Tabletop invitation — A3 or 11×17, landscape, one per station

Background: Clarkson green (dark). Headline in white, subhead in gold, small line in white.

```
                     CAN YOU BREAK THIS AI?

                   First, you'll have to teach it.

       About 3 minutes · No coding · No account · Camera optional

                                              [placeholder: CLARKSON · AI INSTITUTE]
```

Type sizes for A3: headline 160 pt, subhead 60 pt, small line 32 pt. Keep at least 25 mm clear margin. No other text on this sign.

---

## 2. QR sheet — A4 or letter, portrait, one per station (tape flat to the table edge)

```
        Use your phone, or build with us here.

                 [ QR CODE — 60 mm square ]

                 <the https address>

        Works on any phone. Nothing to install. No account.
```

Make the QR code from the https address where this folder is hosted (any static host; the app has to be on https for phone cameras to work). Use a plain black-on-white code at error-correction level M or higher and test it from three metres with two different phones before the event. Print the address under the code in 14 pt so it can be typed.

---

## 3. Exit sign — A4 or letter, landscape, beside the way out of the station

```
        AI gets more powerful.
        Human judgment gets more important.

        Build the Future with AI at Clarkson.
```

First two lines white, 48 pt. Third line gold, 40 pt. Dark green background.

        Images are processed in this browser and are

Plain text, 14 pt, white on dark green or black on white — whichever the sign family uses.
